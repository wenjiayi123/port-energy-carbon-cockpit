# Code of conduct

We expect respectful, technically honest collaboration. Contributors must not harass others,
publish private information, misrepresent benchmark output as production evidence, or conceal
data, safety, licensing, or conflict-of-interest limitations.

Maintainers may edit or remove content and restrict participation when behavior harms people,
the project, or the integrity of its evidence. Report conduct issues privately to the maintainer;
do not open a public issue containing personal or security-sensitive information.
